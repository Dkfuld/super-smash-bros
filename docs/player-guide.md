# Player guide

## Joining

1. Open the link / scan the QR / enter the room code. No app store needed.
   (Optional: "Add to Home Screen" for fullscreen.)
2. Enter a display name, then pick **your** league identity from the 12 names.
3. Build your fighter: body, team color, face, hair, accessory — live 3D
   preview. If you were last place last season, the 🌈 hat is already waiting.
4. Test your controls, hit **I'M READY**, keep the page open.
5. Rotate to **landscape** when the match starts (the game reminds you).

## Controls (touch)

- **Left thumb** — virtual joystick, appears wherever you touch.
- **👊 ATTACK** — tap. With a weapon, uses the weapon; hold for stream weapons
  (leaf blower, hot sauce).
- **💥 HOLD** — hold to charge a heavy attack, release to unleash. Longer
  charge = more damage and comedy knockback.
- **💨 DODGE** — quick roll with invincibility frames. Has a short cooldown.
- **⬆️ JUMP** — hop over low obstacles, onto crates/stools/the stage.
- **🖐️ GRAB** — pulses gold when a dropped weapon/power-up is near.
- Settings ⚙️: left-handed layout, sensitivity, aim assist, auto-face, haptics.

Keyboard (desktop): WASD/arrows move, **J** attack, **K** heavy (hold), **L** or
Shift dodge, **Space** jump, **E** grab, **Q** emote. Gamepads work too.

## How to not get pick 12

- The match starts calm — **loot first**. Supply drops fall with a rarity beam
  (gray→green→blue→gold→purple ["Extremely Questionable"]).
- Watch the floor: red rings mean a hazard is about to trigger. Move.
- The **AUTO-DRAFT zone** eats the arena from the outside. Outside the circle
  you take growing damage after a short grace. The red screen edge = you're in it.
- Knockback kills: near the edge, one Inflatable Mallet ends your season.
  Dodge has i-frames; getting knocked down grants brief invincibility on wakeup.
- If you disconnect, reopen the same link fast — an AI babysits your fighter
  for 20 seconds, keeping your health, weapon and spot.

## When you're eliminated

You see your draft pick immediately and stay in as a spectator. The first
player out gets pick 12, the last one standing gets pick 1. Yes, it's binding.
Yes, everyone saw it. Yippee!
